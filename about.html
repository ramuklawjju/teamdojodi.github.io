Our story

DoJodi began in 2014 with a single belief: planning an event should feel as good as the event itself. A decade and more than 600 weddings, launches and celebrations later, we are a tight-knit studio that still plans every event as if it were our own — calm, organised and a little bit obsessed with the details.

Aanya Mehta

Founder & Creative Director
Aanya founded DoJodi in 2014 after ten years running large-format conferences across three continents.

She set out to build the kind of studio she wished she'd been able to hire: one that treats logistics as a craft and design as a promise. Today she leads creative direction on every signature event, shaping the look and the guest experience from the first conversation.

Aanya is happiest with a floor plan and a strong coffee, untangling the kind of timing problem that makes an event feel effortless to everyone else in the room.

Daniel Okoye

Head of Production
Daniel runs the moving parts — suppliers, build schedules, sound, light and the run-of-show that keeps the day on time.

With a background in live production and photography, he sees an event as a series of cues that have to land in the right order. He builds the timeline, briefs every supplier, and is on site from the first delivery to the last van out.

If something can go wrong, Daniel has already planned for it twice. Clients rarely meet the contingency plans — which is exactly the point.

Mei Lin

Design & Styling Director
Mei turns a brief into a feeling — through colour, florals, lighting and the hundred styling choices guests sense but never see.

Trained in set design, she leads the studio's creative styling, working with florists, lighting designers and fabricators to make each space feel intentional from the entrance to the last table.

She believes the best design is the kind nobody can quite explain — it just feels right the moment you walk in.
