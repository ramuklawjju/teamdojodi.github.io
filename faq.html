General

We are a full-service event and wedding planning studio. That means we can take an event from a first idea all the way to teardown — design, budgeting, suppliers, timelines and on-the-day coordination — or step in for just the parts you need help with.

Whichever way you work with us, you get one dedicated planner as your single point of contact.

Weddings and engagements, milestone birthdays and anniversaries, private parties, and corporate events such as launches, conferences and team celebrations. If it has guests, a budget and a date, we can help — anywhere from 20 to 500+ people.

Our studio is in Indiranagar, Bangalore, and we plan events across India. We also take on a small number of destination events each year — if your venue is further afield, just ask and we'll talk through travel and logistics.

Planning & booking

For weddings and larger events we recommend 9 to 12 months, mostly so you have the pick of venues and dates. Smaller celebrations often come together in 6 to 8 weeks. Short notice? Get in touch anyway — we keep some capacity for last-minute events.

You can choose a signature package or build your own plan from individual services. After a short discovery call we send a clear, itemised proposal — no hidden fees. A deposit secures your date, with the balance split across agreed milestones.
